What is Sam & Sky?

The Story

Between our leagues, our Discord, and the events we run through Eventbrite, this site is the one-stop hub for Arsenal supporters across Toronto. We began this journey in May 2026, when Sam and Aakash started organizing meetups to bring the Gooners of Toronto together: watch parties to rejoice the wins, games before kickoff, prize announcements, and no shortage of Arsenal songs along the way.

What You Get

Two competitive FPL leagues to join (General Classic and the Tdot Gooners League), with two prizes up for grabs in the Classic League — one for the League and one for the Cup. Join the Discord server for live matchday banter and meetups through Eventbrite to watch, play, and celebrate together in person. Simple, free, and built around one thing: bringing Toronto Gooners together, on and off the pitch.
